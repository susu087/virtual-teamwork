How to read the components

Log changes add across the three factors.

1

Activity: GDP per person

2

Energy intensity: total energy supply per unit GDP

3

Carbon intensity: territorial CO₂ per unit energy supply

GDP per person is PPP-adjusted; energy supply is from OWID; CO₂ covers territorial fossil-fuel and industrial emissions. This mixed-source accounting decomposition is descriptive, not causal.
Data, definitions & methodology

Sources

World Bank and Our World in Data GDP per capita (PPP), Our World in Data / Global Carbon Budget territorial CO₂ per capita, OWID energy supply and mix, and World Bank World Development Indicators sector value-added shares. Dataset notes and citations are in data/SOURCES.md.

Country sample

GDP and CO₂ records are joined on ISO-3 code and year. Aggregate OWID rows are excluded from country-level models. Charts use only rows with both values; the displayed sample size can therefore vary by year.

Comparison groups

All development-group comparisons use IMF WEO October 2024: advanced economies (Developed) versus emerging market and developing economies (Developing). The classification is fixed across years. Unclassified economies remain available in global views but are excluded from both development groups.

Derived measures

Index = 100 × value in year ÷ value in baseline year. YoY (%) = 100 × (value in year ÷ value in prior year − 1). CO₂ intensity = CO₂ per person ÷ GDP per person.

Limits

Territorial fossil/industrial CO₂ excludes land-use change and emissions embodied in imports. PPP GDP is in constant international dollars. Missing observations are not imputed. Correlation is not causation.
